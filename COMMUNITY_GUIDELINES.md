## Our Promise

LYNQURA is built on trust, respect, and safety. We're here to support each other, not judge, diagnose, or harm.

---

## What We Expect

### 1. Be kind and respectful
- Speak to people like they matter—because they do
- Assume good intent
- Remember: everyone is fighting a battle you know nothing about

### 2. Share from your experience, not as an expert
- "This helped me" is powerful. "You should do this" can be harmful
- Peer support means lived experience, not professional advice
- Don't diagnose, prescribe medications, or claim to be a licensed professional if you're not

### 3. Respect boundaries
- If someone sets boundaries (No advice, No DMs, No links), honor them
- If someone asks for comfort, don't give advice
- If someone wants to be left alone, respect that

### 4. Respect privacy
- Don't share other people's personal info (names, locations, workplaces, etc.)
- Don't screenshot and share conversations without consent
- What's said in LYNQURA stays in LYNQURA

### 5. Ask before giving advice
Even well-meaning advice can feel overwhelming. Try:
- "Would advice help, or do you just need to be heard?"
- "Want to know what worked for me, or just comfort?"

## What's Not Allowed

### Harassment and hate
❌ Bullying, threats, intimidation, or stalking  
❌ Hate speech based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any protected characteristic  
❌ Sexual harassment or unwanted romantic/sexual advances  

### Harmful content
❌ Encouraging self-harm, suicide, or dangerous behaviors  
❌ Sharing methods or instructions for self-harm  
❌ Glorifying eating disorders, substance abuse, or risky activities  

### Exploitation
❌ Sexual content, nudity, or solicitation  
❌ Predatory behavior (manipulating vulnerable users)  
❌ Attempting to build romantic/sexual relationships under the guise of support  

### Spam, scams, and misuse
❌ Spam, ads, or unsolicited promotions  
❌ Phishing, scams, or fraudulent schemes  
❌ Collecting users' personal info without consent  
❌ Impersonating others or creating fake profiles  

### Medical and legal advice
❌ Telling people to start or stop medication  
❌ Diagnosing conditions ("You have depression")  
❌ Giving legal advice  

### Unapproved links and content
❌ Sharing unapproved links (only curated Support Cards allowed unless post boundaries permit)  
❌ Malicious links, viruses, or malware  

### Misuse of features
❌ Abusing the reporting system (false reports)  
❌ Creating multiple accounts to evade bans

## How to Support Others Safely

### ✅ Do this
- "I hear you."
- "That sounds really hard."
- "You're not alone."
- "What would feel supportive right now?"
- Share what worked for you (if they're open to it)
- Send a Support Card (calm reset, song, resource)

### ❌ Don't do this
- "You're overreacting."
- "Just think positive."
- "Stop your meds and try [alternative thing]."
- "You should…" (without asking first)
- Give medical or legal advice

## What LYNQURA Is (and Isn't)

### LYNQURA is:
✅ Peer support and daily wellness tools  
✅ A place to feel less alone  
✅ Topic-based Hubs for real conversations  
✅ Trusted Supporters who check in on you  

### LYNQURA is NOT:
❌ Therapy or professional mental health treatment  
❌ Emergency services or crisis intervention  
❌ A place to get diagnosed or prescribed medication  

**If you're in immediate danger:**  
Call **911** (U.S./Canada) or your local emergency number  
Or call/text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, U.S./Canada)

## Reporting and Safety

### If you see something unsafe
Use the **Report** button on any post, comment, or profile.

**Report immediately if:**
- Someone is encouraging self-harm or suicide
- You see threats, harassment, or hate speech
- Someone is sharing explicit content
- You suspect scams, spam, or predatory behavior

### If you're struggling
- Use the **SOS button** for crisis resources
- Reach out to your Trusted Supporters
- Lower your Pulse score to 1–3 if you want support
- **If in immediate danger, call emergency services**

### Blocking and muting
You can block or mute any user anytime. You don't need a reason.

## Consequences for Violations

If you violate these Guidelines, we may:
1. **Warning:** We'll let you know what went wrong
2. **Temporary mute:** You can't post or message for a set time
3. **Suspension:** Your account is paused (can be appealed)
4. **Permanent ban:** Your account (and associated devices/IDs) is removed

**For serious violations (threats, exploitation, illegal activity), we may ban immediately and report to law enforcement.**

### Appeals
If you believe your account was suspended or content removed unfairly, contact [support@gotu.com].
