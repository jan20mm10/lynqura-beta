## LYNQURA Is Not Emergency Services

**If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services right now.**

- **U.S. and Canada:** Call **911**
- **Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (U.S./Canada):** Call or text **988**
- **Crisis Text Line (U.S./Canada):** Text HOME to **741741**

LYNQURA is a peer support community. We cannot respond to emergencies in real time.

---

## What LYNQURA Provides

### Peer support
You can connect with others who understand what you're going through. But peer supporters are not licensed therapists or crisis counselors.

### Pulse check-ins
Every 15 minutes during active use, LYNQURA will ask how you're feeling (1–10). This helps you stay aware of your emotional state and lets your Trusted Supporters know when you might need extra care.

### Trusted Supporters
You can choose up to 5 Trusted Supporters. If your Pulse score is 1–3 **and you consent**, they'll receive a notification: "Someone you support needs a check-in now."

**They won't see:**
- Your exact Pulse score
- Private journals or messages
- Where you are or what you're doing

### Crisis resources
If you're in distress, LYNQURA will offer:
- Links to crisis hotlines and text lines
- Calming resets (breathing exercises, grounding techniques)
- Encouragement to reach out to emergency services or a professional

## What LYNQURA Does NOT Provide

❌ Real-time crisis intervention  
❌ 24/7 monitoring or guaranteed response times  
❌ Professional therapy or counseling  
❌ Medical advice or treatment  
❌ Diagnosis of mental health conditions  

**LYNQURA is not a substitute for emergency services, therapy, or medical care.**

## When Your Pulse Is Low (1–3)

If your Pulse score is 1–3, here's what happens:

### 1. You'll see a Safety Screen
**Message:** "I'm here with you. You matter."

**Options:**
- Alert my Trusted Supporters (yes/no)
- View crisis resources
- Start a 2-minute calming reset
- Message a moderator (if available)

**Important:** If you're in immediate danger, we'll encourage you to contact emergency services.

### 2. Trusted Supporter notification (only if you choose "Yes")
Your Trusted Supporters will receive a simple message: "Someone you support needs a check-in now."

They can respond with:
- "I'm here."
- "Want to talk?"
- "Breathe with me" (link to calming exercise)

### 3. Safety Ticket is created
A Safety Ticket is sent to LYNQURA's moderation team. This helps us:
- Identify users who may need additional resources
- Review for imminent harm (threats to self or others)
- Follow up when moderators are available

**We do not guarantee real-time response.** If you need immediate help, call emergency services.

## Content That Signals Imminent Harm

If you post or message content that suggests you might harm yourself or someone else, LYNQURA may:

### 1. Surface urgent resources
You'll see crisis hotlines, text lines, and emergency service reminders

### 2. Slow down posting
We may add a brief friction ("Are you sure you want to post this?") to give you a moment to pause

### 3. Create a high-priority Safety Ticket
A moderator will review as soon as possible

### 4. Contact authorities (in extreme cases)
If we believe there's imminent risk of serious harm and we have enough information, we may contact emergency services or law enforcement

**We take this step only in rare, extreme situations where we believe it's necessary to protect life.**

## Trusted Supporters: What to Know

### If you're a Trusted Supporter
Thank you for being there. Here's how to help safely:

**✅ Do:**
- Respond with kindness: "I'm here with you."
- Ask what they need: "Do you want comfort, advice, or distraction?"
- Encourage professional help if they're in crisis: "Can you call 988 or talk to a therapist?"
- Send a Support Card (calm reset, song, affirmation)

**❌ Don't:**
- Try to "fix" them
- Give medical advice or tell them to stop medication
- Promise you'll always be available (you're not a crisis hotline)
- Feel responsible for their safety (you're offering peer support, not clinical care)

**If you're overwhelmed:**
- You can pause Trusted Supporter alerts anytime in Settings
- You can remove yourself from someone's Trusted Supporter list
- You can message a LYNQURA moderator for guidance

## Reporting Concerning Content

### If you see something unsafe
Use the **Report** button immediately if you see:
- Threats of self-harm or suicide
- Plans or methods for harming self or others
- Harassment, bullying, or exploitation
- Spam, scams, or illegal activity

**Don't wait.** Every report is reviewed.

### What happens after you report
1. The report is added to our moderation queue
2. A moderator reviews it (usually within a few hours, but not guaranteed)
3. We may:
   - Send the user crisis resources
   - Reach out to offer support
   - Suspend or remove the account (for severe violations)
   - Contact authorities (in rare, extreme cases)

## Boundaries and Self-Care

### You are not responsible for others' safety
Peer support is powerful, but it has limits. You are **not** responsible for preventing someone else's crisis or harm.

### Protect your own well-being
- Set boundaries: use "No DMs" or pause Trusted Supporter alerts if you need space
- Block or mute users who drain you
- Take breaks from LYNQURA when needed
- Seek professional help if supporting others is affecting your own mental health

---

## What LYNQURA Moderators Can (and Can't) Do

### We can:
- Review Safety Tickets and reported content
- Offer resources and encouragement
- Remove harmful content or accounts
- Escalate to authorities in extreme situations

### We can't:
- Provide therapy or counseling
- Monitor all conversations in real time
- Guarantee we'll see or respond to every crisis signal immediately

**If you're in crisis, don't wait for a moderator. Call emergency services.**

By using LYNQURA, you agree that:
- You're responsible for your own safety and well-being
- LYNQURA is not a substitute for professional care
- You'll seek help from a therapist, doctor, or crisis hotline when you need it
- You won't hold LYNQURA responsible for outcomes of peer interactions

## Privacy and Confidentiality

### What we keep confidential
Most Safety Tickets and moderation actions are private. We don't publicly share details about users in crisis.

### When we may break confidentiality
We may disclose information if:
- We believe there's imminent risk of serious harm to self or others
- We're required by law (court order, subpoena)
- We're cooperating with law enforcement to prevent harm
