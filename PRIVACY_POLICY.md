## 2. Information We Collect

### A) Information you give us directly

**Account creation (private, not shown to others):**
- Full legal name
- Date of birth (to verify you're 18+)
- Address
- Email or phone number
- Government-issued ID (for verification only)

**Public profile (shown to other users):**
- Display name (nickname)
- Avatar
- Optional bio
- Topics you're open to discussing

**When you use LYNQURA:**
- Check-ins ("How are you, really?")
- Pulse scores (1–10 self-reports)
- Journal entries (private unless you share)
- Posts, comments, and messages
- Support Request Types (comfort, advice, distraction, etc.)
- Boundaries you set on posts
- Trusted Supporters list (up to 5)
- Topics and Hubs you engage with
- Premium subscription status
- Blocked users list

### B) Information we collect automatically

**Device & usage data:**
- Device type, operating system, browser
- IP address, general location (city/region, not precise GPS)
- App activity (screens viewed, time spent, features used)
- Crash reports and performance data

**Cookies & similar technologies:**
- We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand how LYNQURA is used
- You can control cookies in your browser settings

### C) Information from third parties

**ID verification:**
- We use a third-party service to verify your identity (name, DOB, address, ID photo)
- This provider processes data on our behalf and deletes it after verification

**Payment processors:**
- If you subscribe to Premium, Stripe or Apple/Google handles payment info
- We never see your full credit card number

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

### Keep LYNQURA safe
- Verify you're 18+
- Enforce Community Guidelines
- Detect and prevent harassment, spam, fraud, or harmful behavior
- Review Safety Tickets when Pulse scores are low (1–3)
- Respond to reports and moderate content

### Make LYNQURA work

- Match you with peers based on topics and preferences
- Show you relevant Hubs, resources, and resets
- Send notifications (Pulse reminders, supporter alerts, messages)
- Manage blocked users and safety features
- Provide Premium features if you subscribe

### Improve LYNQURA
- Understand how people use the app (what's helpful, what's confusing)
- Fix bugs and improve performance
- Test new features
- Analyze trends (like "Stress Hub is growing" or "Pulse check-ins help retention")

### Communicate with you
- Send account updates, safety alerts, policy changes
- Respond to your support requests
- Occasional product updates (you can opt out)

### Legal obligations
- Comply with laws, court orders, or legal processes
- Protect rights, safety, and property

## 4. How We Share Your Information

**We don't sell your data. Ever.**

We only share your information in these specific situations:

### With other LYNQURA users (limited, consent-based)
- **Your public profile:** nickname, avatar, bio, topics (visible to users you interact with)
- **Your posts/comments:** visible based on your visibility settings (Trusted Supporters, Friends, Hub, or Private)
- **Trusted Supporter alerts:** if your Pulse is 1–3 **and you consent**, your Trusted Supporters see: "Someone you support needs a check-in now" (no private details)

### With service providers (who work for us)
- ID verification (to confirm you're 18+)
- Hosting and cloud storage (Firebase/Google Cloud)
- Chat infrastructure (Stream Chat or similar)
- Payment processing (Stripe, Apple, Google)
- Email/SMS delivery (Twilio, SendGrid, or similar)
- Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics)
- Customer support tools

**These providers:**
- Process data only as instructed by LYNQURA
- Are contractually required to protect your data
- Cannot use your data for their own purposes

### With moderators and safety staff
- Internal moderators/admins can access Safety Tickets, reports, and flagged content
- They see only what's necessary to review safety concerns
- They're bound by confidentiality agreements

### For legal reasons
We may disclose information if required by law or if we believe in good faith it's necessary to:
- Comply with legal process (subpoena, court order, warrant)
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect safety (imminent harm to self or others)
- Investigate fraud, abuse, or violations

### If LYNQURA changes hands
If we're acquired, merge, or sell assets, your information may transfer to the new owner (you'll be notified)

### Aggregated/de-identified data
We may share anonymized, aggregated data publicly or with partners (example: "80% of users report feeling calmer after 7 days") — this data cannot identify you

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

You have control over your data.

### Access your data
You can view and download most of your data in Profile → Settings → Your Data

### Edit your information
Update your public profile, topics, and preferences anytime in Settings

### Delete your account
You can delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete Account

**What happens when you delete:**
- Your public profile, posts, and comments are permanently removed
- Your private data (legal info, Pulse scores, journals) is deleted within 30 days
- Some data may be retained for legal/safety reasons (like reports of Terms violations)

### Control notifications
Turn off Pulse reminders, Supporter alerts, or marketing emails in Settings → Notifications

### Opt out of marketing
Click "unsubscribe" in any marketing email, or adjust preferences in Settings

### Trusted Supporters
Add, remove, or pause alerts anytime in Connect → Trusted Supporters

### Visibility and boundaries
Control who sees each post (Trusted Supporters, Friends, Hub, Private) and set boundaries (No advice, No DMs, No links, Keep it gentle)

## 6. Data Security

We take security seriously:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access controls:** Only authorized personnel can access sensitive data
- **Monitoring:** We monitor for suspicious activity and unauthorized access
- **Incident response:** If a breach occurs, we'll notify affected users as required by law

**But no system is 100% secure.** If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at [security@lynqura.com].
